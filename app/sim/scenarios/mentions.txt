# --- Silent mode "for me only": a busy channel stays dark, a mention or a direct message wakes the screen
key s
expect-screen Settings
key .*10
shot m8_silent_setting
key /
shot m9_silent_for_me
expect-setting wake 1
key `
screen off
rxch Public Alice nothing for you here
wait 500
expect-woke 0
expect-sounds 5
rxch Public Alice @[Raphael] this one is for you
wait 500
expect-woke 1
expect-sounds 6
screen off
rx Bob a direct message
wait 500
expect-woke 2
expect-sounds 7
